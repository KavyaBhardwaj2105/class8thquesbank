Section G — HOTS / Exam Challenge

46. A 7.5 m ribbon is cut into pieces of 0.5 m each. How many pieces are obtained?

Correct Answer: 15 pieces. 7.5 ÷ 0.5 = 15.

47. A rectangular field is 12.5 m long and 8.4 m wide. Find its area.

Correct Answer: 105 m². 12.5 × 8.4 = 105.

48. A bottle contains 2.4 L of juice. It is poured equally into 6 glasses. How much juice does each glass receive?

Correct Answer: 0.4 L.

49. A student scores 72 out of 100. Express the score as a decimal and percentage.

Correct Answer: 0.72 and 72%.

50. A ₹1200 bag is sold at a 15% discount. Find its selling price.

Correct Answer: ₹1020. Discount = 15% of ₹1200 = ₹180; selling price = ₹1020.

51. Without using a calculator, decide which is greater: 3.75 × 0.4 or 1.5.

Correct Answer: They are equal because 3.75 × 0.4 = 1.5.

52. A number is multiplied by 0.1 and the result is 4.7. What was the original number?

Correct Answer: 47 because 47 × 0.1 = 4.7.

53. Which is greater: 5/8 or 0.6?

Correct Answer: 5/8, because 5/8 = 0.625 > 0.6.

54. A shopkeeper buys an item for ₹800 and sells it for ₹920. What is the percentage increase based on the original price?

Correct Answer: 15%. Increase = ₹120; 120/800 × 100 = 15%.

55. A student calculates 0.24 ÷ 0.06 as 0.4. Identify the correct result and reason.

Correct Answer: 4. 0.24 ÷ 0.06 = 24 ÷ 6 = 4.
