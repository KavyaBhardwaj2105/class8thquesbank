Correct Answer: B) Diameter

Correct Answer: B) 14 cm

4. Which point is equally distant from every point on a circle?

Correct Answer: B) Centre

5. Two circles having the same centre are called:

Correct Answer: B) Concentric circles

6. How many radii can be drawn in a circle?

Correct Answer: D) Infinitely many

7. If two chords of a circle have equal lengths, they are:

Correct Answer: A) Equidistant from the centre

8. A diameter divides a circle into:

Correct Answer: B) Two semicircles

9. If AB is a diameter and O is the centre, which relation is correct?

Correct Answer: B) AB = 2OA

10. A circle has radius 12 cm. What is its diameter?

Correct Answer: C) 24 cm

11. Which of the following is NOT a chord?

Correct Answer: B) A radius

13. If two chords are at the same distance from the centre, then they are:

Correct Answer: B) Equal in length

14. A circle with centre O has OP = 9 cm and OQ = 9 cm. What can be concluded?

Correct Answer: A) P and Q lie on the same circle centred at O

15. If a chord is 16 cm long and its midpoint is 5 cm from the centre, what does the perpendicular from the centre divide it into?

Correct Answer: B) 8 cm and 8 cm

Section G — HOTS / Exam Challenge

46. A circle has radius 13 cm. A chord is 10 cm long. What is the length of each half of the chord when the perpendicular from the centre bisects it?

Correct Answer: 5 cm.

47. Two chords of the same circle are 14 cm and 14 cm long. Compare their distances from the centre.

Correct Answer: They are equal.

48. Circle A has radius 9 cm and Circle B has radius 9 cm. Their centres are different. What can definitely be said?

Correct Answer: The circles are equal in size.

49. A diameter of a circle is 34 cm. A student says its radius is 68 cm. What is the error?

Correct Answer: Radius = 34 ÷ 2 = 17 cm.

50. A chord is 18 cm long. Its perpendicular distance from the centre meets it at M. What is AM if AB is the chord?

Correct Answer: 9 cm, because the perpendicular from the centre bisects the chord.

51. Three circles have radii 4 cm, 7 cm and 10 cm, all with the same centre. What type of arrangement is this?

Correct Answer: Concentric circles.

52. A circle has radius 15 cm. A chord is 24 cm long. What is the distance from the centre to the midpoint of the chord?

Correct Answer: 9 cm. Half-chord = 12 cm; radius = 15 cm; by right triangle, distance = √(15²−12²)=9 cm.

53. Two chords of a circle are at distances 4 cm and 7 cm from the centre. Which chord is longer?

Correct Answer: The chord closer to the centre is longer.

54. A circle has diameter 50 cm. A chord of length 48 cm is drawn. Find the distance of the chord from the centre.

Correct Answer: 7 cm. Radius = 25 cm, half chord = 24 cm, so distance = √(25²−24²)=7 cm.

55. A student claims that two equal chords can be at different distances from the centre of the same circle. Is the claim correct?

Correct Answer: No, equal chords of the same circle are equally distant from the centre.
