Section F — Drag & Drop Style Questions (5)

41. Arrange the steps for evaluating 3x + 2 when x = 4.

Correct Sequence: x = 4 → 3(4)+2 → 12+2 → 14.

42. Arrange from simplest to most expanded form: 2(x + 3).

Correct Sequence: 2(x + 3) → 2x + 6.

43. Arrange the operations for evaluating 5a − 4 when a = 6.

Correct Sequence: 5×6 → 30 → 30−4 → 26.

44. Choose the correct sequence for finding the perimeter of a square of side x.

Correct Sequence: x+x+x+x = 4x.

45. Arrange the expressions in increasing number of terms.

Correct Sequence: 7x (1 term) → x+3 (2 terms) → 2a+b+5 (3 terms).
Section G — HOTS / Higher Order Thinking (10)

46. A rectangle has length x cm and breadth 5 cm. Which expression gives its perimeter?

Correct Answer: 2(x+5) = 2x+10.

47. A number is represented by n. If 7 is subtracted from it and the result is doubled, which expression represents the result?

Correct Answer: 2(n − 7).

48. If x + 8 = 15, what is the value of 2x?

x = 7, therefore 2x = 14.

49. Two consecutive numbers are represented by n and n+1. Which expression gives their sum?

Correct Answer: n + (n+1) = 2n+1.

50. The cost of one notebook is ₹p. What is the cost of 6 notebooks and a pen costing ₹12?

Correct Answer: 6p + 12.

51. If a = 3 and b = 5, which expression has the greatest value?

Values: 8, 15, 16, 9. Therefore 2a+2b is greatest.

52. A student says 3x + 4x = 7x². Is the student correct?

Correct Answer: No. Like terms are added by adding their coefficients: 3x+4x=7x.

53. The perimeter of an equilateral triangle is 3s. If s = 8 cm, what is the perimeter?

Correct Answer: 3 × 8 = 24 cm.

54. If y = 4, compare 3y + 2 and 2y + 7. Which statement is correct?

Correct Answer: 2y + 7 is greater by 1. 3(4)+2 = 14 and 2(4)+7 = 15.

55. A pattern has 4 matchsticks in Figure 1, 7 in Figure 2 and 10 in Figure 3. Which expression gives the number of matchsticks in Figure n?

Correct Answer: 3n + 1. For n=1,2,3 it gives 4,7,10.
