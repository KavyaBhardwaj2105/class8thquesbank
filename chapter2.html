Section A — Multiple Choice Questions (15)

1. What is the value of 18 + 6 × 4?

Correct Answer: 42
6 × 4 = 24, therefore 18 + 24 = 42.

2. Evaluate: 72 ÷ 8 + 5 × 3.

Correct Answer: 24
72 ÷ 8 = 9 and 5 × 3 = 15. Hence 9 + 15 = 24.

3. Which operation must be performed first in 45 − 12 ÷ 3?

Correct Answer: Division

4. Find the value of (25 − 7) × 3 + 4.

Correct Answer: 58
25 − 7 = 18; 18 × 3 = 54; 54 + 4 = 58.

5. Which expression has the value 30?

Correct Answer: (8 + 4) × 2 + 6
8 + 4 = 12; 12 × 2 = 24; 24 + 6 = 30.

6. Evaluate: 100 − [24 ÷ (3 + 1)].

Correct Answer: 94
3 + 1 = 4; 24 ÷ 4 = 6; 100 − 6 = 94.

7. If a = 8, what is the value of 5a + 12?

Correct Answer: 52
5 × 8 + 12 = 40 + 12 = 52.

8. Which expression represents “subtract 9 from the product of 7 and 6”?

Correct Answer: (7 × 6) − 9

9. What is the value of 36 ÷ 3 × 2?

Correct Answer: 24
Division and multiplication have equal precedence and are evaluated from left to right.

10. Evaluate: 7 + 3 × (18 − 10).

Correct Answer: 31
18 − 10 = 8; 3 × 8 = 24; 7 + 24 = 31.

11. Which pair of brackets changes 20 + 8 ÷ 4 into an expression having value 7?

Correct Answer: (20 + 8) ÷ 4

12. Find the missing number: 9 × □ − 7 = 56.

Correct Answer: 7
9 × 7 = 63 and 63 − 7 = 56.

13. Which expression correctly represents “half of the sum of 18 and 10”?

Correct Answer: (18 + 10) ÷ 2

14. Evaluate: 144 ÷ [6 × (5 − 1)].

Correct Answer: 6
5 − 1 = 4; 6 × 4 = 24; 144 ÷ 24 = 6.

15. If x = 12 and y = 5, what is the value of 3x − 2y?

Correct Answer: 26
3(12) − 2(5) = 36 − 10 = 26.
Section B — True / False (10)

16. In an arithmetic expression without brackets, multiplication is generally performed before addition.

Correct Answer: True

17. The value of 40 − 6 × 3 is 102.

Correct Answer: False
6 × 3 = 18, therefore 40 − 18 = 22.

18. Brackets can change the value of an arithmetic expression.

Correct Answer: True

19. 48 ÷ 6 × 2 = 4 because multiplication must always be performed before division.

Correct Answer: False
48 ÷ 6 = 8; 8 × 2 = 16.

20. The expression 7 × (4 + 3) has value 49.

Correct Answer: True

21. 36 − 12 ÷ 3 = 8.

Correct Answer: False
12 ÷ 3 = 4, so 36 − 4 = 32.

22. The expression 15 + 20 ÷ 5 has value 7.

Correct Answer: False
20 ÷ 5 = 4, so 15 + 4 = 19.

23. An expression can contain more than one pair of brackets.

Correct Answer: True

24. 5 + 3 × 2 and (5 + 3) × 2 have the same value.

Correct Answer: False
The first equals 11, while the second equals 16.

25. In 64 − 8 × 4, the multiplication is completed before subtraction.

Correct Answer: True

26. Assertion: 12 + 4 × 5 = 32.
Reason: Multiplication is performed before addition.

Correct Answer: A

27. Assertion: (15 + 5) × 2 = 40.
Reason: Brackets are evaluated before multiplication.

Correct Answer: A

28. Assertion: 36 ÷ 6 × 3 = 18.
Reason: Division and multiplication have equal precedence and are evaluated from left to right.

Correct Answer: A
36 ÷ 6 = 6, and 6 × 3 = 18. Both the Assertion and Reason are true, and the Reason correctly explains the calculation.

29. Assertion: 50 − (12 + 8) = 30.
Reason: The expression inside the brackets is evaluated first.

Correct Answer: A

30. Assertion: 8 + 12 ÷ 4 = 5.
Reason: Division is performed before addition.

Correct Answer: D
Division is performed first, but the expression equals 11, not 5.

41. Arrange the operations of 18 + 6 × 4 − 5 in the correct order.

Correct Order: Multiplication → Addition → Subtraction

42. Arrange the steps for 90 − [24 ÷ (2 + 4)] from first to last.

Correct Order: (2 + 4) → 24 ÷ 6 → 90 − 4

43. Which operation should replace □ so that 8 □ 4 × 3 = 20?

Correct Answer: +
4 × 3 = 12 and 8 + 12 = 20.

44. Choose the correctly ordered expression for: “Add 7 to 5, then multiply the result by 4.”

Correct Expression: (5 + 7) × 4

45. Which arrangement of operations makes 6 + 3 × 2 equal to 12?

Correct Arrangement: 6 + 3 × 2 = 12
Section G — HOTS (10)

46. Riya calculates 24 + 6 × 5 as 150, while Aman calculates it as 54. Who is correct?

Correct Answer: Aman
6 × 5 = 30 and 24 + 30 = 54.

47. An expression has value 36: 8 + 4 × □. What number should replace the box?

Correct Answer: 7
8 + 4 × 7 = 8 + 28 = 36.

48. Which expression has a value greater than 50?

Correct Answer: 9 × 6 − 2
9 × 6 − 2 = 54 − 2 = 52.

49. Two expressions are E₁ = 7 × (10 − 4) and E₂ = 7 × 10 − 4. How much greater is E₂ than E₁?

Correct Answer: 24
E₁ = 7 × 6 = 42.
E₂ = 70 − 4 = 66.
Difference = 66 − 42 = 24.

50. A shopkeeper charges ₹15 per notebook and ₹20 as a fixed packing charge. Which expression gives the cost of n notebooks?

Correct Answer: 15n + 20

51. Which pair of expressions is guaranteed to have the same value for every number x?

Correct Answer: 2(x + 5) and 2x + 10
Using distributive property: 2(x + 5) = 2x + 10.

52. A student changes 48 ÷ (4 + 2) into 48 ÷ 4 + 2. What happens to the value?

Correct Answer: It changes from 8 to 14.
48 ÷ (4 + 2) = 48 ÷ 6 = 8.
48 ÷ 4 + 2 = 12 + 2 = 14.

53. Find the smallest whole number k for which 5k + 7 is greater than 42.

Correct Answer: 8
For k = 7, 5k + 7 = 42, which is not greater than 42.
For k = 8, 5k + 7 = 47.

54. A number puzzle follows the rule: multiply the number by 3, then subtract 5. What is the output when the input is 17?

Correct Answer: 46
17 × 3 = 51; 51 − 5 = 46.

55. Which expression has the greatest value?

Correct Answer: (50 − 4) × 6
50 − 6 × 4 = 26
(50 − 6) × 4 = 176
(50 − 4) × 6 = 276
50 + 6 × 4 = 74
