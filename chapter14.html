Reason: It passes through the midpoint of the segment.

Correct Answer: A

27. Assertion: Every point on a perpendicular bisector is equally distant from both endpoints.
Reason: The perpendicular bisector is perpendicular to the segment.

Correct Answer: B — both are true, but perpendicularity alone does not explain the equal-distance property.

28. Assertion: Six equilateral triangles can meet around a point without gaps.
Reason: Each equilateral triangle has an angle of 60°.

Correct Answer: A

29. Assertion: Four squares can meet at one point in a tiling.
Reason: Each square contributes 90° at that point and 4 × 90° = 360°.

30. Assertion: Regular pentagons alone form a gap-free edge-to-edge tiling.
Reason: Their interior angle is 108°, and 108° does not divide 360° exactly.

Correct Answer: D — Assertion is false; Reason is true.

Section G — HOTS / Exam Challenge

46. A student wants to construct the perpendicular bisector of AB. Why are equal-radius arcs drawn from A and B?

Correct Answer: Their intersection points are equally distant from A and B.

47. Can regular hexagons tile a plane by themselves? Explain using angles.

Correct Answer: Yes. Three regular hexagons meet at a point: 3 × 120° = 360°.

48. A regular polygon has interior angle 108°. How many such angles would be needed to reach 360°?

Correct Answer: None exactly, because 360 ÷ 108 is not a whole number.

49. A square tiling has 4 squares meeting at each interior vertex. What is the total angle around that vertex?

Correct Answer: 360°.

50. A point P lies on the perpendicular bisector of AB. If PA = 13 cm, what is PB?

Correct Answer: 13 cm.

51. A regular hexagon is used for tiling. Three hexagons meet at a vertex. What is the angle contributed by each?

Correct Answer: 120°.

52. A regular polygon has 8 sides. Its interior angle is 135°. Can identical regular octagons tile the plane by themselves?

Correct Answer: No. 360° cannot be made by whole-number multiples of 135°.

53. A construction requires a point that is equally distant from two given points A and B. Where should the point lie?

Correct Answer: On the perpendicular bisector of AB.

54. A tiling uses equilateral triangles. At one vertex, 5 triangles meet. Is there a gap or overlap?

Correct Answer: A gap of 60°. Five × 60° = 300°, leaving 60°.

55. A designer wants a regular polygon to tile the plane by itself. Which test is most useful?

Correct Answer: Check whether a whole number of its interior angles add exactly to 360°.
