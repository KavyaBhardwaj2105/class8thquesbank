Section A — Multiple Choice Questions

1. When two lines intersect, how many angles are formed?

Correct Answer: 4

2. If one angle formed by two intersecting lines is 68°, its vertically opposite angle is:

Correct Answer: 68°

3. The sum of angles in a linear pair is:

Correct Answer: 180°

4. Three lines intersecting at one point form how many sectors around that point?

Correct Answer: 6

5. Two adjacent angles are 73° and x°. If they form a linear pair, x equals:

Correct Answer: 107°

6. An angle is 132°. Its adjacent angle in a linear pair is:

Correct Answer: 48°

7. If one angle formed by two intersecting lines is 90°, the other three angles are:

Correct Answer: 90°, 90°, 90°

8. If two adjacent angles are equal and form a straight angle, each angle measures:

Correct Answer: 90°

9. Three concurrent lines divide the complete angle around their common point into:

Correct Answer: 6 sectors

10. If one angle at an intersection is 47°, the angle adjacent to it in a linear pair is:

Correct Answer: 133°

11. Three lines pass through the same point. How many pairs of vertically opposite angles are formed?

Correct Answer: 3 pairs

12. The angles around a single point have a total measure of:

Correct Answer: 360°

13. If x° and (x + 40)° form a linear pair, x is:

Correct Answer: 70°

14. If an angle is 50°, its vertically opposite angle is:

Correct Answer: 50°

15. Which statement is always true for vertically opposite angles?

Correct Answer: They are equal

Section B — True / False

16. Vertically opposite angles are equal.

Correct Answer: True

17. A straight angle measures 90°.

Correct Answer: False — it measures 180°.

18. Angles around a point add up to 360°.

Correct Answer: True

19. The angles of a linear pair have a sum of 180°.

Correct Answer: True

20. Three concurrent lines create four sectors at their common point.

Correct Answer: False — they create six sectors.

21. Vertically opposite angles can measure 70° and 110°.

Correct Answer: False

22. The two angles of a linear pair need not be equal.

Correct Answer: True

23. If one angle at an intersection is 115°, its vertically opposite angle is 65°.

Correct Answer: False — it is 115°.

24. A linear pair consists of adjacent angles whose non-common arms form a straight line.

Correct Answer: True

25. Three concurrent lines produce three pairs of vertically opposite angles.

Correct Answer: True

Section G — HOTS

46. Two adjacent angles form a linear pair and are in the ratio 1 : 2. Find both angles.

Correct Answer: 60° and 120°.

47. Three concurrent lines form six angles in order as a, b, c, a, b, c. If a = 40° and b = 60°, find c.

Correct Answer: 80°.

48. Three lines intersect at one point. Three consecutive angles are x, 2x and 3x. Find x.

Correct Answer: 30°.

49. Two adjacent angles are (3x + 10)° and (2x + 20)°. They form a linear pair. Find the two angles.

Correct Answer: 100° and 80°.

50. Two lines are perpendicular. A third ray divides one right angle into 35° and x°. Find x.

Correct Answer: 55°.

51. Two adjacent angles are (4x + 5)° and (2x + 55)°. They form a linear pair. Find x.

Correct Answer: 20.

52. Six angles made by three concurrent lines occur as a,b,c,a,b,c. If a=72° and b=48°, find c.

Correct Answer: 60°.

53. Three consecutive sectors formed by three concurrent lines are in the ratio 2 : 3 : 4. Find the three sector angles.

Correct Answer: 40°, 60°, 80°.

54. A student says: “If one angle formed by two intersecting lines is 125°, all four angles are 125°.” Which conclusion is correct?

Correct Answer: 125°, 55°, 125°, 55°.

55. Two adjacent angles differ by 36° and form a linear pair. Find the smaller angle.

Correct Answer: 72°.
