Section G — HOTS / Higher Order Thinking (10)

46. Riya ate 2/5 of a cake and her brother ate 1/4 of the same cake. What fraction of the cake was eaten altogether?

Correct Answer: 13/20.

47. A tank is 5/6 full. If 1/3 of the tank's capacity is used, what fraction of the tank remains full?

Correct Answer: 1/2. 5/6 − 1/3 = 5/6 − 2/6 = 3/6 = 1/2.

48. A ribbon is 7/8 metre long. It is cut into pieces of 1/8 metre each. How many pieces are obtained?

Correct Answer: 7 pieces.

49. A student has ₹240 and spends 3/8 of it. How much money is left?

Correct Answer: ₹150. Spent = 3/8 × 240 = ₹90, so remaining = ₹150.

50. Which is greater: 5/8 or 7/12, and by how much?

Correct Answer: 5/8 is greater by 1/24.

51. A recipe needs 3/4 cup of flour. Meera makes half the recipe. How much flour does she need?

Correct Answer: 3/8 cup.

52. A path is 2 1/2 km long. A person walks 1 3/4 km. How much farther must the person walk?

Correct Answer: 3/4 km.

53. A number is multiplied by 3/5 and the result is 18. What is the original number?

Correct Answer: 30. Number = 18 ÷ 3/5 = 18 × 5/3 = 30.

54. A jar contains 3/4 kg of sugar. It is divided equally among 6 packets. How much sugar is in each packet?

Correct Answer: 1/8 kg.

55. A student solves 2/3 + 3/4 as 5/7. What is the main error in the student's method?

Correct Answer: A common denominator is required. 2/3 = 8/12 and 3/4 = 9/12, so the correct sum is 17/12.
