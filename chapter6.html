Section A — Multiple Choice Questions (15)

Q1. Which of the following numbers is divisible by 3?

Correct Answer: 5,436. Its digit sum is 18, which is divisible by 3.

Q2. Which number is divisible by both 2 and 5?

Correct Answer: 6,750.

Q3. A number is divisible by 9 when:

Correct Answer: the sum of its digits is divisible by 9.

Q4. Which digit can replace □ in 4,52,7□6 so that the number is divisible by 3?

Correct Answer: 0. The digit sum is 4+5+2+7+0+6 = 24, which is divisible by 3.

Q5. Which pair contains numbers having the same remainder when divided by 5?

Correct Answer: 37 and 52. Both leave remainder 2 when divided by 5.

Q6. What is the remainder when 347 is divided by 10?

Correct Answer: 7.

Q7. Which expression always gives an even number for every integer n?

Correct Answer: 2n.

Q8. If a number leaves remainder 4 when divided by 7, which could be that number?

Correct Answer: 46, because 46 = 7 × 6 + 4.

Q9. Which number is divisible by 4?

Correct Answer: 5,732. Its last two digits, 32, are divisible by 4.

Q10. Which number is divisible by 6?

Correct Answer: 2,418. It is even and its digit sum is 15.

Q11. What is the smallest positive number divisible by both 4 and 6?

Correct Answer: 12.

Q12. A number is divisible by 5 if its last digit is:

Correct Answer: 0 or 5.

Q13. If 8,472 is divided by 8, the remainder is:

Correct Answer: 0, because 8,472 ÷ 8 = 1,059.

Q14. Which statement about a prime number is correct?

Correct Answer: It has exactly two positive factors: 1 and itself.

Q15. Which of the following is a factor of 84?

Correct Answer: 7, because 84 = 7 × 12.
Section B — True / False (10)

Q16. Every multiple of 10 is also a multiple of 5.

Correct Answer: True.

Q17. Every number divisible by 3 is also divisible by 6.

Correct Answer: False.

Q18. The remainder after division is always smaller than the divisor.

Correct Answer: True.

Q19. 1 is a prime number.

Correct Answer: False. 1 has only one positive factor.

Q20. The product of two odd numbers is always odd.

Correct Answer: True.

Q21. The sum of two odd numbers is always odd.

Correct Answer: False. Odd + odd = even.

Q22. A number ending in 0 is divisible by 10.

Correct Answer: True.

Q23. 48 is divisible by both 4 and 8.

Correct Answer: True.

Q24. 91 is divisible by 7.

Correct Answer: True, because 91 = 7 × 13.

Q25. If two numbers have the same remainder when divided by 4, their difference is divisible by 4.

Correct Answer: True.
Section C — Assertion & Reasoning (5)
Options:
A = Both A and R are true, and R correctly explains A.
B = Both A and R are true, but R does not explain A.
C = A is true, but R is false.
D = A is false, but R is true.

Q26. Assertion: 246 is divisible by 2.
Reason: A number ending in an even digit is divisible by 2.

Correct Answer: A.

Q27. Assertion: 135 is divisible by 5.
Reason: The digit sum of 135 is 9.

Correct Answer: B. Both statements are true, but the digit-sum rule does not explain divisibility by 5.

Q28. Assertion: 72 is divisible by 8.
Reason: The last three digits of a number determine divisibility by 8.

Correct Answer: A.

Q29. Assertion: 37 is a prime number.
Reason: Every odd number is prime.

Correct Answer: C.

Q30. Assertion: If two numbers leave the same remainder when divided by 5, their difference is divisible by 5.
Reason: Numbers with the same remainder differ by a multiple of the divisor.

Correct Answer: A.

Section F — Drag & Drop Style Questions (5)

Q41. Select the number that is divisible by 9.

Correct Answer: 7,245. Its digit sum is 18.

Q42. Select the number that is a multiple of both 4 and 5.

Correct Answer: 320.

Correct Answer: 31, because 31 = 6 × 5 + 1.

Section G — HOTS (10)

Q46. A two-digit number is divisible by both 3 and 5. Which could be the number?

Correct Answer: 75.

Q47. Find the number between 40 and 60 that leaves remainder 2 when divided by 5 and remainder 1 when divided by 4.

Correct Answer: 57. 57 ÷ 5 leaves remainder 2 and 57 ÷ 4 leaves remainder 1.

Q48. Three consecutive integers have a sum of 72. What is the middle integer?

Correct Answer: 24. The numbers are 23, 24 and 25.

Q49. A number gives remainder 3 when divided by 8. Which number could it be?

Correct Answer: 51, because 51 = 8 × 6 + 3.

Q50. Which is the smallest number greater than 100 that is divisible by both 4 and 9?

Correct Answer: 108. 108 is divisible by 4 and its digit sum is 9.

Q51. The sequence 2, 5, 8, 11, ... follows which rule?

Correct Answer: Add 3 each time.

Q52. Which number must be added to 68 to make the result divisible by 9?

Correct Answer: 4. 68 + 4 = 72, and 72 is divisible by 9.

Q53. Two numbers have a difference of 18. Both leave the same remainder when divided by 6. What can be concluded?

Correct Answer: Their difference is divisible by 6.

Q54. A number is divisible by 3 and 4. Which of the following must also divide the number?

Correct Answer: 12. A number divisible by both 3 and 4 is divisible by their LCM, 12.

Q55. A number is divisible by 4 and 6 but not by 8. Which could be the number?

Correct Answer: 12. It is divisible by 4 and 6, but not by 8.
