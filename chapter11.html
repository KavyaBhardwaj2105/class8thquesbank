Section C — Assertion & Reasoning

Correct Answer: A

27. Assertion: The LCM of 8 and 12 is 24.
Reason: 24 is the smallest positive number divisible by both 8 and 12.

Correct Answer: A

28. Assertion: 35 and 64 are co-prime.
Reason: Their only common factor is 1.

Correct Answer: A

29. Assertion: Prime factorisation helps find HCF and LCM.
Reason: Common prime factors help determine HCF and the highest powers of prime factors help determine LCM.

Correct Answer: A

30. Assertion: The LCM of 9 and 15 is 45.
Reason: 45 is a common multiple of 9 and 15.

Correct Answer: B — both statements are true, but merely being a common multiple does not establish that 45 is the least one.

Section G — HOTS / Exam Challenge

46. Two ropes are 84 m and 126 m long. They must be cut into equal pieces of maximum possible length. What should be the length of each piece?

Correct Answer: 42 m. HCF(84,126)=42.

47. Three lights blink every 8, 12 and 18 seconds. If they blink together now, after how many seconds will they blink together again?

Correct Answer: 72 seconds. LCM(8,12,18)=72.

48. Find the HCF and LCM of 45 and 75.

Correct Answer: HCF = 15 and LCM = 225.

49. A 120 cm × 168 cm rectangular sheet is to be divided into the largest possible identical squares. What is the side of each square?

Correct Answer: 24 cm. HCF(120,168)=24.

50. If HCF of two numbers is 6 and their LCM is 180, what is the product of the numbers?

Correct Answer: 1080 because HCF × LCM = product.

51. Two numbers have HCF 8 and LCM 120. If one number is 24, find the other.

Correct Answer: 40. 8 × 120 = 24 × other number.

52. A student says the prime factorisation of 72 is 6 × 12. Is the statement complete?

Correct Answer: B. 72 = 2³ × 3².

53. Find the smallest number divisible by 6, 8 and 15.

Correct Answer: 120. LCM(6,8,15)=120.

54. The HCF of 48 and 72 is 24. Without calculating LCM directly, find it.

Correct Answer: 144. LCM = (48 × 72) ÷ 24 = 144.

55. A school has 96 boys and 72 girls. They want to form the greatest possible number of identical groups, with the same number of boys and girls in every group. How many groups can be formed?

Correct Answer: 24 groups. HCF(96,72)=24.
