Section G — HOTS (10)

46. A submarine is at −85 m. It rises 37 m. What is its new position?

Correct Answer: −48 m.

47. The temperature is −6°C at night and rises by 14°C in the morning. What is the morning temperature?

Correct Answer: 8°C.

48. A game gives +6 points for a correct answer and −4 for an incorrect answer. A player gets 7 correct and 3 incorrect. Find the score.

Correct Answer: 30. Score = 7×6 − 3×4 = 42−12 = 30.

49. Simplify: −18 + 7 − 12 + 25.

Correct Answer: 2.

50. A bank account has a balance of −₹750. A deposit of ₹1,200 is made. What is the new balance?

Correct Answer: ₹450.

51. A lift starts at floor −3, moves up 8 floors, then down 5 floors. At which floor does it stop?

Correct Answer: 0. −3+8−5=0.

52. Find the missing integer: −15 + x = 9.

Correct Answer: x=24.

53. A number is multiplied by −7 and the result is 56. What is the number?

Correct Answer: −8, because (−8)×(−7)=56.

54. Without calculating every step, determine the sign of (−3)×(−5)×(−2).

Correct Answer: Negative. There are three negative factors, an odd number.

55. A student says: −12−8 = −4 because 12−8=4. What is the correct explanation?

Correct Answer: −20. Subtracting 8 from −12 moves 8 more units left on the number line.
