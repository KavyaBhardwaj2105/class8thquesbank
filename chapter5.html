Section A — Multiple Choice Questions (15)

1. Two lines that meet at exactly one point are called:

Correct Answer: Intersecting lines.

2. Parallel lines remain:

Correct Answer: The same distance apart.

3. Two lines forming an angle of 90° are:

Correct Answer: Perpendicular.

4. How many points do two distinct parallel lines have in common?

Correct Answer: No point.

5. Which symbol represents two lines being parallel?

Correct Answer: ∥

6. If two lines intersect, the point where they meet is called the:

Correct Answer: Point of intersection.

7. Which pair of objects best represents parallel lines?

Correct Answer: Opposite edges of a ruler.

8. If one line crosses two parallel lines, it is called a:

Correct Answer: Transversal.

9. Two perpendicular lines form how many right angles at their intersection?

Correct Answer: Four.

10. If two parallel lines are cut by a transversal, corresponding angles are:

Correct Answer: Equal.

11. Vertically opposite angles formed by two intersecting lines are:

Correct Answer: Equal.

12. If one angle at an intersection is 65°, its vertically opposite angle is:

Correct Answer: 65°.

13. If two adjacent angles form a straight line and one is 72°, the other is:

Correct Answer: 108°, because 180° − 72° = 108°.

14. Which condition proves that two lines are perpendicular?

Correct Answer: They meet at 90°.

15. If one of two supplementary angles is 127°, the other is:

Correct Answer: 53°.
Section B — True / False (10)

16. Two parallel lines never intersect.

Correct Answer: True.

17. Perpendicular lines always make an angle of 90°.

Correct Answer: True.

18. Intersecting lines must always be perpendicular.

Correct Answer: False. Intersecting lines can form angles other than 90°.

19. Vertically opposite angles are equal.

Correct Answer: True.

20. Two distinct parallel lines have one common point.

Correct Answer: False.

21. A transversal can intersect two parallel lines.

Correct Answer: True.

22. Adjacent angles on a straight line add up to 180°.

Correct Answer: True.

23. Corresponding angles are equal when a transversal cuts parallel lines.

Correct Answer: True.

24. A right angle measures 180°.

Correct Answer: False. A right angle measures 90°.

25. Two lines can intersect at more than one point and still be distinct straight lines.

Correct Answer: False.
Section C — Assertion & Reasoning (5)

A = Both true and R explains A | B = Both true but R does not explain A | C = A true, R false | D = A false, R true

26. Assertion: Vertically opposite angles are equal.
Reason: They are formed by the same two intersecting lines on opposite sides.

Correct Answer: A.

27. Assertion: Parallel lines do not meet.
Reason: The perpendicular distance between them remains constant.

Correct Answer: A.

28. Assertion: Two intersecting lines can form four right angles.
Reason: This happens when the lines are perpendicular.

Correct Answer: A.

29. Assertion: If one angle of a linear pair is 40°, the other is 140°.
Reason: Angles forming a linear pair have a sum of 180°.

Correct Answer: A.

30. Assertion: Corresponding angles are equal even when the two lines are not parallel.
Reason: Equality of corresponding angles can be used to establish that two lines are parallel.

Correct Answer: C. The assertion is false, while the reason is true.

Correct Answer: Parallel.

32. The angle between perpendicular lines is ______.

Correct Answer: 90°.

Correct Answer: Transversal.

34. The sum of angles in a linear pair is ______.

Correct Answer: 180°.

Section F — Drag & Drop Style Questions (5)

41. Arrange the reasoning steps to find the missing angle if a linear pair contains 68°.

Correct sequence: Linear pair → 180° → 180°−68° = 112°.

42. Arrange the steps for finding the vertically opposite angle to 83°.

Correct sequence gives 83°.

43. Arrange from smallest angle to largest angle.

Correct Sequence: 30°, 45°, 90°, 120°.

44. Choose the correct sequence for identifying perpendicular lines.

Correct Sequence: Lines meet → angle 90° → perpendicular.

Section G — HOTS / Higher Order Thinking (10)

46. Two parallel lines are cut by a transversal. One corresponding angle is 74°. What is the other corresponding angle?

Correct Answer: 74°.

47. Two lines intersect. One angle is 118°. Find the adjacent angle.

Adjacent angles form a linear pair: 180°−118° = 62°.

48. A transversal makes an angle of 56° with one of two parallel lines. What is the corresponding angle at the other line?

Correct Answer: 56°.

49. Two angles form a linear pair. Their measures are (3x + 10)° and (2x + 20)°. Find x.

3x+10+2x+20=180 → 5x=150 → x=30.

50. At an intersection, one angle is twice another angle. If the two are adjacent, what are their measures?

Let the smaller angle be x. x+2x=180°, so x=60°. The angles are 60° and 120°.

51. A student says two intersecting lines are parallel because they look almost parallel. What is the best mathematical response?

Correct Answer: Parallelism must be established by geometric properties.

52. If two lines are perpendicular, what is the measure of every angle formed at their intersection?

Correct Answer: 90°.

53. Corresponding angles made by a transversal are 3x° and (x + 40)°. If the lines are parallel, find x.

Corresponding angles are equal: 3x=x+40 → 2x=40 → x=20.

54. Two adjacent angles are (4x − 10)° and (2x + 10)°. They form a straight line. Find the larger angle.

4x−10+2x+10=180 → 6x=180 → x=30. Angles are 110° and 70°. Larger angle = 110°.

55. A pair of corresponding angles are (5x − 15)° and (3x + 25)°. If the lines are parallel, what is the common angle?

5x−15 = 3x+25 → 2x=40 → x=20. Common angle = 5(20)−15 = 85°.
